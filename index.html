03 / The practical guide

How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet.

Treat a spreadsheet as a research starting point. Confirm the details on the current listing before making a purchase.

01

Build a focused shortlist

Choose a category and save a few relevant products. Record the exact variant, listing link, stated price and date checked so similar items stay easy to distinguish.

02

Compare the evidence

Review the size chart, materials and available product photos. If quality check photos are available, confirm that they match the item and variant you are considering.

03

Check the purchase route

Confirm current availability, seller terms and the destination site's buying instructions. If using a shopping service, verify that it accepts the listing and product type.

05 / Clear answers

Before you
follow a find.

A few useful distinctions when researching an Oopbuy product list, checking clothing measurements or comparing sneaker finds.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research list associated with searches for shopping through Oopbuy. Depending on its creator, it may organize product links, categories, prices, sizes or photo references. Entries must be checked against the current product listing.

Can I order products on this page?

This page is an independent browsing guide, not a checkout or a downloadable spreadsheet. Its category links open CAFANS. Check the destination listing and its purchase instructions before ordering.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact product variant, measurements, materials, available photos, current price and purchase conditions. If quality check photos are available, confirm that they correspond to the relevant item and variant.

Does a product link confirm authenticity or availability?

No. A product link, brand name or illustration does not confirm authenticity, authorization, stock or compatibility with a shopping service. Verify these details with the relevant seller or service.

What costs should I check before ordering?

Check the item price, applicable domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international shipping and any taxes or duties. The final total depends on the seller, service, parcel and destination.
